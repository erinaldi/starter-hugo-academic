abstract: "We use the Logarithmic Linear Relaxation (LLR) density of states algorithm to study the bulk phase transitions of pure-gauge SU($N$) lattice Yang--Mills theories with $4 \\leq N \\leq 8$. This approach avoids super-critical slowing down at such transitions, which poses a problem for traditional importance sampling Monte-Carlo methods. We analyse the effect of different updating strategies within the LLR algorithm, different reconstruction techniques of the density of states and different lattice volumes. By comparing our results for the weakly first-order SU(5) bulk phase transition against those for the stronger transitions with $N \\geq 6$, we demonstrate the advantages of the LLR method for analyses of strong transitions with large latent heat."
